# Tabletop scenario: compromised employee account and property access

Status: **PREPARED — NOT YET EXERCISED**

## Scenario

A cleaner reports that a work phone is missing after a phishing prompt was
opened. The phone may contain an active Dar Tahara session, a WhatsApp thread
with customer details and an appointment-limited smart-lock credential for a
property scheduled later that day. A customer then reports an unexpected access
notification. Production WhatsApp is currently inactive, so the facilitator
must adapt that element to the approved communication channel if this remains
true on the exercise date.

## Injects and decisions

1. **T+0:** lost device/phishing report. Who declares severity and owns the case?
2. **T+10:** active session and property appointment identified. Demonstrate
   account/session revocation, staff deactivation and lock/key revocation.
3. **T+20:** possible customer-property access. Decide customer contact,
   physical response, evidence preservation and law-enforcement criteria.
4. **T+35:** logs are split across Supabase, receiver and suppliers. Preserve
   event IDs, timestamps and hash-chain evidence without collecting extra PII.
5. **T+50:** possible personal-data breach and foreign processors. Escalate to
   Legal/Privacy/CNDP decision-making; do not invent a notification deadline.
6. **T+70:** device recovered. Decide forensics, credential rotation, return to
   service, employee support/discipline and corrective actions.

## Expected evidence

Attendees and roles; timeline; severity decision; revocation evidence; customer
and authority decision log; legal questions; control failures; action owners and
dates. The scenario is complete only after the actual session and approved
minutes are recorded.
