Evidence state: **DESIGNED/POPULATED — APPROVAL AND OPERATION OPEN**

role and location data; populated asset, supplier-review, feature, data-flow and
risk registers; an updated provisional Statement of Applicability; and a
Morocco legal-obligations register.

gates rather than blank values. Morocco Law 09-08/CNDP requirements, the 2026
tax-code accounting candidate and conditional GDPR applicability are separated
from proposed business/security periods. No database retention rule was enabled
and real-record deletion remains disabled.

property-access/account-compromise tabletop and role-based employee training
material are prepared. These are not approved objectives, completed exercises,
delivered training, independent audit evidence or management-review minutes.
