# Internal audit program

Status: **DRAFT — INDEPENDENT AUDITOR APPOINTMENT REQUIRED**

The program covers the approved ISMS scope, ISO/IEC 27001 clauses, applicable Annex A controls, legal/contractual requirements and Dar Tahara procedures. Frequency and depth are risk-based, considering change, incidents, supplier dependency, prior findings and control performance.

The auditor must demonstrate competence and independence from the work audited. Planning defines criteria, scope, sample and evidence access. Findings distinguish conformity, nonconformity and improvement, identify objective evidence, owner, due date and corrective action. Management tracks root cause, correction, effectiveness and overdue actions. Results and trends are mandatory management review inputs. No blank template or self-review by the control implementer is claimed as an internal audit.
