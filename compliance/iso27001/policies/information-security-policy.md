## Purpose and scope

Dar Tahara will operate an information security management system aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 for the approved scope. Confidentiality, integrity and availability requirements apply to people, suppliers, software, cloud services, property-access information and customer records.

## Commitments

Management must approve objectives, risk criteria, resources, roles and residual risk. Control owners must maintain evidence, report events promptly, meet legal and contractual obligations, measure control performance and correct nonconformities. Access is least-privilege and reviewed; changes are tested and traceable; incidents, continuity, suppliers and records are managed through the linked procedures.

No system or person is exempt without a documented, time-limited risk exception approved by the risk owner. Personnel acknowledgement and an annual or material-change review are required after approval.
