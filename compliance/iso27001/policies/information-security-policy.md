## Purpose and scope

Dar Tahara will operate an information security management system aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 for the approved scope. Confidentiality, integrity and availability requirements apply to people, suppliers, software, cloud services, property-access information and customer records.

## Commitments

Management must approve objectives, risk criteria, resources, roles and residual risk. Control owners must maintain evidence, report events promptly, meet legal and contractual obligations, measure control performance and correct nonconformities. Access is least-privilege and reviewed; changes are tested and traceable; incidents, continuity, suppliers and records are managed through the linked procedures.

No system or person is exempt without a documented, time-limited risk exception approved by the risk owner. Personnel acknowledgement and an annual or material-change review are required after approval.

## Integrity and ethical conduct

Personnel and service providers acting for Dar Tahara must be honest, lawful and
accurate in security, privacy, customer, operational and evidence records. They
must not conceal control failures, incidents, exceptions, conflicts of interest
or mistakes; falsify evidence; bypass approved controls; misuse access; retaliate
against good-faith reporting; or make unsupported security, availability,
confidentiality, privacy or compliance claims.

Suspected misconduct, conflicts, security events and control failures must be
reported promptly through the approved reporting or management escalation path.
Good-faith reports are handled confidentially to the extent reasonably possible
and must not lead to retaliation. Management, HR and Legal must define the final
investigation, disciplinary and whistleblowing arrangements under applicable
law before this section becomes operational.

Managers and control owners must model the approved requirements, communicate
them to personnel, investigate deviations consistently, preserve relevant
evidence, record corrective action and escalate material matters to the approved
oversight body. Commercial targets or delivery urgency do not authorize a
control bypass or inaccurate record.

## Communication, acknowledgement and review

After approval, the policy owner issues the controlled version to every in-scope
role and identifies any role-specific procedures. Personnel must acknowledge the
policy before receiving independent privileged, customer-property or restricted
information access and again after a material change. The policy owner reviews
completion and overdue acknowledgements at least monthly until complete, then
as part of the quarterly control review.

Acknowledgement confirms receipt and understanding; it does not waive employee
rights or replace training. Actual acknowledgement records remain in the
approved restricted evidence store, with only sanitized references in the
repository.
