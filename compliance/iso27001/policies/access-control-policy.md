# Access control policy

Status: **DRAFT — MANAGEMENT APPROVAL REQUIRED**

Owner / approver / effective date / review date: **TBD**

Access must be uniquely attributable, least-privilege, role-based and approved by the information owner. Privileged access requires AAL2, dedicated administrative use, logged break-glass handling and periodic review. Shared accounts are prohibited unless a documented technical constraint, named custodian, rotation and compensating log exist.

Joiner, mover and leaver changes must be ticketed, approved, verified and retained as evidence. Access is revoked immediately on termination or material loss of need. Supplier and service accounts receive the same lifecycle controls. Property keys/codes follow the property-access procedure. Quarterly privileged and supplier reviews are proposed and require management approval before becoming the operating cadence.
