# Acceptable use policy

Status: **DRAFT — MANAGEMENT/HR APPROVAL REQUIRED**

Personnel may use Dar Tahara information and systems only for authorized business purposes. Customer, employee, property-entry, payment and credential information must not be copied to personal accounts, unmanaged devices or unapproved AI/file-sharing services. Credentials may not be shared. Security controls may not be bypassed.

Personnel must lock devices, apply updates, use approved storage and communication channels, verify unusual payment/access requests, and immediately report suspected phishing, loss, disclosure or unauthorized access. Monitoring is limited to approved security and operational purposes and remains subject to applicable law and notice requirements. HR/Legal must approve sanctions, monitoring language and acknowledgement evidence.
