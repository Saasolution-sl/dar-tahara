# Records retention policy

Status: **DRAFT — LEGAL/PRIVACY/RECORD-OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED**

Retention periods must be recorded by category with legal basis, owner, approval date and review date. No period in code, an environment variable or this draft substitutes for Legal/Privacy approval. Deletion must cover the system of record, objects, caches and relevant suppliers; backups expire through their approved lifecycle rather than selective alteration.

Bulk deletion fails closed when a rule is missing, disabled, unapproved or overdue, when an applicable legal hold exists, or when execution is not explicitly enabled. Legal holds are authorized, traceable and released only by an approved role. Execution evidence records category, mode, result and errors without storing record content.
