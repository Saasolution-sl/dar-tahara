# Supplier security policy

Status: **DRAFT — LEGAL/PROCUREMENT APPROVAL REQUIRED**

Every production supplier requires an owner, data/service classification, security and privacy due diligence, contract/DPA references, subprocessors and transfer review, breach notification terms, access boundaries, continuity dependency, review date and exit/deletion plan. Higher-risk suppliers require current assurance evidence and tracked findings before use or renewal.

No register entry is evidence that a contract or assurance report exists. The supplier review procedure and register distinguish confirmed evidence from required follow-up. Material supplier changes, incidents or assurance expiry trigger an out-of-cycle review.
