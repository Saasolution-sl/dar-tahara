# Recovery and exercise plan

Status: **DRAFT — APPROVED BIA AND LIVE EXERCISE REQUIRED**

Recovery priority follows the approved BIA. The incident lead declares disruption, records the start, protects evidence, invokes supplier/escalation contacts, authorizes restore/failover, communicates status and validates business service before closure. Technical recovery uses the encrypted off-site backup/isolated restore runbook; property access and billing require approved manual workarounds.

At least one measured exercise must cover database/object restoration plus a critical supplier or communications loss. Capture detection, decision, restore, data currency, integrity, service validation, communications, achieved RTO/RPO and corrective actions. Never use production customer data in an uncontrolled exercise.
