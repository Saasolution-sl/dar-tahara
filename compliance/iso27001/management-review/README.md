# Management review

No management review has occurred. `review-template.md` now captures required
inputs/outputs, and `corrective-action-register.csv` tracks ownership and
effectiveness. They remain blank templates; management decisions, approvals and
minutes must be recorded by authorized people after the independent audit.

The SOC 2 Security and Controls Oversight Committee defined in
`../../frameworks/soc2/controls-oversight-charter.md` provides recurring
internal-control challenge. Its meetings do not automatically constitute the
formal ISO/IEC 27001 management review. A formal management review must still
cover all required inputs and outputs and retain its own approved minutes.
