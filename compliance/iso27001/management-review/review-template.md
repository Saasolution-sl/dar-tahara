# ISMS management review template

Status: **BLANK TEMPLATE — NOT MANAGEMENT REVIEW EVIDENCE**

Record date, attendees, authority/quorum and prior-action status. Inputs must cover context and interested parties, ISMS scope, objectives/metrics, risk and treatment, audit results, incidents/nonconformities, monitoring/control drift, vulnerabilities, supplier performance, continuity exercises, resources/competence, legal changes and improvement opportunities.

For each decision record owner, resources, due date and acceptance criteria. Outputs include changes to the ISMS, objectives/controls, resources and approved residual risk. Attach approved minutes and link corrective actions; signatures/approval may not be inferred from attendance.
