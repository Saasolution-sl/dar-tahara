# Supplier security and review procedure

Status: **DRAFT — LEGAL/PROCUREMENT APPROVAL REQUIRED**

Before onboarding or renewal, the service owner completes `supplier-review-register.csv`: purpose, data, access, hosting/transfer locations, criticality, DPA/contract, subprocessors, assurance, incident terms, continuity, retention/deletion and exit. Legal/Privacy decides contractual acceptability; Security tracks assurance findings; the business owner accepts residual risk.

High-risk gaps block production use unless a named risk owner grants a time-limited exception. Evidence is referenced from restricted storage, never committed. Review occurs at the approved cadence and after a material change, incident, assurance expiry or exit. Exit verifies account revocation, data return/deletion, integrations/secrets removal and continuity transition.
