# Initial role-based security-awareness programme

Status: **TRAINING MATERIAL PREPARED — DELIVERY/ACKNOWLEDGEMENT PENDING**

## All personnel

- Customer confidentiality and minimum-necessary access.
- Phishing, social engineering, password sharing and MFA fatigue.
- Approved devices/channels; screen lock, updates and lost/stolen-device action.
- Immediate incident reporting without blame or concealment.
- Secure remote work, exports, photographs and disposal.

## Cleaners and operations personnel

- Information visible inside customer homes must not be photographed, copied or
  discussed unless the approved service purpose requires it.
- Before/after and damage photographs use only the approved application,
  required framing and purpose; never personal galleries or private messaging.
- Never share physical keys, access codes, smart-lock invitations or customer
  information. Verify appointment, property and identity before access.
- Prefer individually attributable, time-limited smart-lock access; report a
  missing key, unexpected access alert or unauthorized person immediately.
- Do not retain customer information or credentials after the task/expiry.

## Technical and privileged personnel

- Least privilege, separate identities, MFA, break-glass and quarterly review.
- Secret handling, phishing-resistant authentication and service-key rotation.
- Protected change workflow, staging/production separation and synthetic tests.
- Security logging, evidence minimization, supplier access and incident custody.

## Evidence and assessment

Deliver in the personnel's working language. Require attendance, dated
acknowledgement and scenario questions with an agreed pass threshold and
follow-up for missed answers. Use `training-register-template.csv`; this outline
is not evidence that training occurred.
