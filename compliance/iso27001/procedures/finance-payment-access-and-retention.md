# Finance, payment access and retention procedure

Status: **DRAFT — FINANCE/LEGAL APPROVAL REQUIRED**

Finance assigns named Stripe and billing roles by least privilege, requires MFA, reviews privileged access, separates refund/adjustment approval where staffing permits, and logs emergency actions. Payment card data is handled by Stripe; Dar Tahara must not store full card numbers or security codes. Disputes, suspected fraud and payment-data events follow defined escalation paths.

Finance and Legal must approve invoice, authorization, refund, dispute and accounting retention categories before deletion is enabled. Continuity covers access to billing records, provider outage and manual reconciliation. Required evidence: role export, review/sign-off, sampled refund/dispute trace, approved schedule and recovery test.
