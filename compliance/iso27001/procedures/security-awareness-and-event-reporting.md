# Security awareness and event reporting

Status: **DRAFT — HR/SECURITY APPROVAL REQUIRED**

Induction and recurring training covers customer/property confidentiality, phishing and payment fraud, credential/MFA handling, approved devices/channels, supplier/AI restrictions and immediate event reporting. Role modules cover administrators, Finance, Operations/property access, Engineering and incident responders.

HR/Security records attendance, assessment, failed-topic follow-up and acknowledgement in `training-register-template.csv`; blank templates are not training evidence. Personnel report suspected loss, phishing, disclosure, malware, unsafe property access or control failure through the approved channel without investigating on their own. Security triages under the incident-response procedure. Management must set cadence, pass threshold and reporting channels.
