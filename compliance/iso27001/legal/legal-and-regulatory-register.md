# Legal and regulatory obligations register

This register is not legal advice. Statutory interpretation, filings and final
retention periods require qualified Moroccan counsel or the appointed
Legal/Privacy owner.

| [CNDP notification procedures](https://www.cndp.ma/procedures-de-notification-process/) | Customer, employee, authentication, marketing, photographs and access data | Determine declarations versus prior authorizations, especially sensitive/identity data and interconnected files | LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED |
| [CNDP foreign-transfer procedure](https://www.cndp.ma/transfert-de-donnees-a-letranger/) | Vercel, Supabase and other suppliers may process outside Morocco | Map recipient countries and legal mechanism; underlying processing must be notified/authorized before the foreign-transfer request | LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED |
| [Moroccan General Tax Code 2026](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/dgi/2025/CGI-2026-FR.pdf), Article 211 | Invoices, accounting books, sales copies and supporting expense/investment documents | Ten-year retention is recorded as a statutory candidate for qualifying accounting records only; Finance/Legal must confirm exact scope and trigger | LEGAL/FINANCE REVIEW REQUIRED |
| [Moroccan Commercial Code](https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2024/03/01/Code%20de%20commerce_compressed-1709282723074.pdf), Article 26 | Commercial correspondence | Ten-year retention candidate for original received and copied sent commercial correspondence; counsel must classify in-scope records | LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED |
| [EU GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679), Articles 3, 5 and 17 | Conditional: EU establishment, offering/monitoring in the EU, or contractual applicability | Determine territorial applicability; if applicable, implement storage limitation, erasure and recipient propagation without displacing Moroccan obligations | LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED |
