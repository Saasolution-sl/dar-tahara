- decision and effective date;
- name, competence, independence and conflict assessment for the independent
  member;

- approved charter version; and
- approved minutes and action follow-up after the meeting.

The independent internal auditor is a separate role. The same person may not
independently audit work they designed or operated, and appointment requires a
documented competence/independence assessment.
