# Security and Controls Oversight Committee charter

Status: **PROPOSED — MANAGEMENT APPROVAL AND INDEPENDENT MEMBER APPOINTMENT REQUIRED**

Control: `DT-GOV-008`

Framework mapping: SOC 2 CC1.2, CC1.5, CC4.1 and CC4.2; ISO/IEC 27001:2022
clauses 5.1, 9.2, 9.3 and 10.

## Purpose

The Security and Controls Oversight Committee is Dar Tahara's proportionate
board-equivalent oversight body during the pre-incorporation and early-stage
period. It challenges management on the design, implementation and operation of
internal control over security, availability and confidentiality. It does not
replace the future Moroccan entity's legally required governing bodies or the
independent internal auditor/CPA.

The charter must be reconciled with the final SARL governance arrangements and
legal advice after incorporation.

## Proposed composition

- **Executive chair:** Othman Deraz, proposed temporary chair and accountable
  executive. The chair supplies management information, resources and decisions
  but is not treated as independent from management.
- **Independent oversight member:** one named person who is not responsible for
  designing or operating the controls under review and who can challenge the
  chair. Appointment is required before the committee can claim independent
  oversight.
- **ISMS manager/control owners:** attend for their agenda items and provide
  evidence. They do not satisfy the independent-member requirement.
- **Independent internal auditor, Legal/Privacy, Finance, HR, Operations and
  external specialists:** attend when relevant, without losing or substituting
  their separate responsibilities.

Independence, relevant competence, conflicts, confidentiality and authority to
access evidence must be documented for the independent member. A consultant who
designed or operated a sampled control is not independent for oversight of that
work without an additional independent challenger.

## Authority and responsibilities

The committee may request evidence and management responses, require corrective
actions, escalate overdue or disputed deficiencies and recommend resources or
scope changes. It must:

1. challenge the system boundary, Trust Services Category scope, commitments,
   objectives and risk criteria;
2. review security, availability and confidentiality metrics, incidents,
   complaints, exceptions, control failures and significant changes;
3. review fraud, management-override, supplier, legal/privacy, people,
   continuity and technology risks;
4. review internal-audit independence, scope, findings and remediation without
   directing the auditor's conclusions;
5. track corrective actions, overdue evidence and repeat deficiencies to
   verified closure;
6. challenge whether resources, competence, deputies and segregation are
   proportionate to the organization;
7. escalate inaccurate or unsupported compliance claims; and
8. provide inputs to, but remain distinguishable from, the formal ISO 27001
   management review and the eventual SOC 2 examination.

The committee cannot self-attest SOC 2 compliance, issue an auditor's opinion,
accept risk for an unauthorized owner, override law or fabricate operating
evidence.

## Cadence, quorum and decisions

- A formal meeting occurs at least quarterly and after a material incident,
  scope change, major supplier failure, material control deficiency or auditor
  escalation.
- Until P0 readiness blockers are closed, a monthly remediation review is held;
  it may be shorter but must retain decisions and challenges.
- Quorum requires the executive chair and the independent oversight member. The
  chair alone cannot approve committee minutes or constitute independent
  oversight.
- When the chair, independent member or their work is implicated, that person
  declares the conflict and is recused from the relevant conclusion. Another
  authorized independent person must chair that item.
- Decisions record the matter, evidence considered, challenge, decision,
  dissent, owner, resources, due date and acceptance criteria. Attendance alone
  is not approval.
- Minutes are approved by the chair and independent member within ten business
  days. Sensitive supporting material remains in the restricted evidence store.

## Required inputs and outputs

At minimum, the committee receives the control dashboard, risk/exception and
corrective-action registers, security incidents or a dated zero-state review,
access and supplier reviews, availability/continuity results, legal/privacy and
HR matters, customer-commitment changes, audit results and prior-action status.

Outputs are approved minutes, challenges, decisions, recusals, action owners,
due dates, resource decisions and escalations. Type II evidence begins with the
first quorate meeting and includes every scheduled meeting and action follow-up;
no historical meeting may be backfilled.

## Review

The committee reviews this charter annually and after incorporation, a material
ownership/governance change or auditor feedback. Management approval, member
appointments and each later revision are retained as controlled evidence.
